## What's new
- App Depot now shows every app in the catalog. "This Mac" (the default) lists the apps for this Mac; "All" adds the Android ones, under "Other platforms". Switch with ⌘1 and ⌘2
- Each app shows which platforms it runs on, and the detail page says when it is also on Android
- Android apps can be sent to your phone: "Send to phone" shows a QR code of the download to scan with the phone's camera, with a Copy link button
- App Depot always updates itself last: updating App Depot first installs the other waiting updates ("Updating 2 other apps first"), and its own update never starts while another app is installing

## What's new

- See every app, not just the Android ones: switch between "This phone" and "All" at the top of the list; the counts show how many apps each has, and your choice is remembered
- Apps that run on several platforms appear once, with a badge for each platform (Android, macOS)
- Apps that aren't available for Android are listed under "Other platforms" with "Not on this phone": open their web page or share the link to get them on your Mac
- An app's page shows where else it runs, such as "Also on macOS · 0.15.3", and for Mac-only apps the latest Mac version and its notes
- Updates, Update all and background checks still only touch apps installed on this phone

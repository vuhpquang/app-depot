## What's new

- App icons on every card, in the app header and in the grid, kept on the phone so they show offline
- Grid view: switch between list and grid from the top bar; your choice is remembered
- Search apps by name or description, with or without Vietnamese accents
- Sort by updates first, name or recently released
- Uninstall an app from its page or from the card's menu
- Skip this version: hide an update you don't want until a newer one comes out
- Download size on Install, Update and Update all
- Share an app's download link, or open its release page

## What's new
- A fresh Material 3 Expressive look on every screen
- Home now leads with your updates: one card shows every app with an update, its progress, and a big Update all button
- The app page has a bigger header, version tiles (installed, latest, download size) and one button with a menu for Skip, Uninstall, Share and Release page
- History is grouped by day, and Settings and the lists use rounded connected groups
- Settings → Appearance → Use wallpaper colours, to follow your wallpaper instead of depot green (off by default)
- Installs show a progress notification with one segment per app; on Android 16 it stays at the top as a Live Update
- List / Grid view, History and Settings moved to the ⋮ menu on Home
